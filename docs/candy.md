# Time candy

- **Durations (@14753950-@14755282 †):** CandySelected 1-5 sets CandyAwayTime to 7201 / 14401 / 28801 / 86401 / 259201 s (2 h / 4 h / 8 h / 24 h / 72 h); `CandyOwned[k]` counts them.
- **Credit:** `AwayTimeRewarded = (LoadTime − QuitTime)·HaveYouPlayedBefore + CandyAwayTime` (@20201031 †).
- **Scope (the author, in game, 2026-10-11):** candy credits only the screen you select, not every screen. Candy on Tilling makes crops but does NOT advance the Apothecary brew bars, so potion levels still wait on brewing.
- **Rate, checked live 2026-10-11:** a 24 h candy on Tilling (all 8 plots on Packed Clay) raised Packed Clay 1.474e11 → 9.093e12. That is about 373B/h, against the Tilling plan's 112B per *real* hour away, a ratio of 3.3× ≈ 1 / nonActiveZoneGainPercent (30.5%). **Candy is credited at full rate, not cut by the away-gain %.** So 1 candy hour ≈ 1 / (away %) real hours away on that screen (24 h ≈ 79 h for the author).
- **Second check, Apothecary (2026-10-11):** a 4 h candy selected on Apothecary filled all six brew bars (the slowest needed 3.3 h watching, 9.8 h away) and, with crops and flowers already held, paid 17 potion levels (+4/+3/+3/+2/+3/+2). Candy = full-rate time on the selected screen, i.e. the same as watching it, on two screens now.
- **Combo that works:** candy Tilling to bank the crops, then candy Apothecary to fill the brew bars; each screen needs its own candy.
